Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Version 2.0
This Privacy Policy describes how Closly ("Closly," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information of users ("you" or "your") who use the Closly mobile application ("the App") and related digital wardrobe and fashion services. We are dedicated to maintaining the highest level of privacy and data security.
01

Information We Collect

To deliver a personalized wardrobe management and styling experience, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Profile: Full name, verified email address, date of birth, gender, country, bio, and profile photo.
  • Styling & Onboarding Preferences: Body measurements, sizing (clothing & shoe sizes), body type, skin tone hex values, preferred color palettes, and favorite fashion brands.
  • Digital Wardrobe Items: Garment photos, categories (tops, bottoms, shoes, etc.), colors, purchase prices, and wear frequencies.
  • Social & Outfits ("MyDay"): Outfit photos, captions, item tags, likes, and following relations.
  • Direct Messaging: One-on-one direct messages exchanged with other fashion community members.
02

How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to power and elevate your wardrobe experience:

  • To provide your digital wardrobe inventory and calculate cost-per-wear analytics.
  • To generate comprehensive closet audit statistics (unworn pieces, monthly intake, most worn outfits).
  • To power personalized fashion newsfeeds based on your onboarding style match.
  • To enable secure in-app messaging and community interactions.
  • To track referral links when you choose to tap "Buy" on affiliate fashion products.
03

Data Storage & Safeguards

Your information is stored in secure cloud environments with encrypted network transmissions (TLS/HTTPS). Authentication is guarded using industry-standard JWT authentication and Firebase secure identity verification.

04

Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. We share limited technical data only with trusted infrastructure partners:

  • Firebase (Google): For secure identity management, Google/Apple authentication, and token verification.
  • Affiliate Networks (Awin & Rakuten): Solely for generating tracked outbound purchase deep links when you tap "Buy".
05

Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may at any time:

  • Request complete permanent deletion of your account and all wardrobe data.
  • Request profile data deletion (removes personal details while keeping login credentials).
  • Export or update your onboarding and wardrobe preference details directly in the App.
Account deletion can be triggered directly inside the mobile app settings or via our web deletion request portal.
