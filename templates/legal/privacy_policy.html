Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
This Privacy Policy describes how Closly ("Closly," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information of users ("you" or "your") who use the Closly mobile application ("the App") and related digital wardrobe and fashion services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data securely.

1 Information We Collect

We collect and process personal information necessary to deliver our digital wardrobe and styling services:

  • Account information (name, email address, date of birth)
  • Profile details (gender, country, bio, profile photo)
  • Onboarding and fashion preferences (body measurements, sizes, style match, skin tone, color palette, preferred brands)
  • Closet and outfit data (uploaded clothing images, categories, wear counts, outfit pictures)
  • Direct messages and community interactions
  • Authentication tokens (Firebase social sign-in or JWT)

2 Purpose of Processing

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide digital wardrobe management, wear tracking, and closet audit analytics
  • To personalize your fashion newsfeed and style matching
  • To enable outfit sharing ("MyDay") and direct messaging with other users
  • To power affiliate product discovery and track referral link clicks
  • To secure accounts and prevent fraud

3 Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely. We maintain appropriate administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

4 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers as needed to operate the App:

  • Firebase (for authentication and security)
  • Affiliate Networks (such as Awin and Rakuten, solely for tracked referral link clicks when you choose to tap 'Buy')

5 Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or permanently delete your personal information. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time via the App settings or through our account deletion request form.

6 Children's Privacy

Closly is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
