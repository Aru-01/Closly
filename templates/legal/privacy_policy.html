01

Introduction & Overview

Closly ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related fashion styling services.

By using Closly, you acknowledge that you have read and consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as data gathered automatically when you interact with the app:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, profile picture, gender preferences, and optional bio/country details.
  • Wardrobe & Fashion Content: Photos of your clothing items, category tags, colors, patterns, brands, outfit logs, style DNA quiz answers, and stories.
  • Social & Messaging Data: Outfits you like or save, comments, followers, following connections, and direct messages exchanged with other fashion community members.
  • Device & Technical Information: Operating system version, device model, IP address, unique app installation identifiers, crash reports, and app performance analytics.
03

Device Permissions & Purpose

To enable core functionality, Closly requests specific device permissions. We access these hardware features strictly with your explicit consent:

  • Camera: Used exclusively when you choose to scan clothes using our AI smart scanner, take a profile picture, or photograph your daily outfit (OOTD).
  • Photo Library: Used to allow you to select and upload existing images of your wardrobe pieces and save outfit collages back to your device.
  • Microphone: Used only if you record video stories or outfit video reels with audio inside the app.
04

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To manage your digital closet, calculate your wardrobe sustainability score, and organize your clothing items.
  • To generate personalized daily outfit suggestions and weather-informed styling recommendations.
  • To power our community newsfeed, stories, and real-time chat between users.
  • To curate tailored product recommendations in the "For You" shopping tab.
  • To maintain system security, detect fraudulent activities, and troubleshoot application performance.
05

AI Image Scanning & Computer Vision

When you use the AI Camera Scanner, images of your clothing are analyzed by our computer vision models to identify garment categories, colors, patterns, and seasons. Photos uploaded for wardrobe scanning are stored securely and are never sold to external data brokers or used for facial recognition.

06

Affiliate Links & Third-Party Partners

myclosly features curated fashion pieces from partner retailers and affiliate networks (such as AWIN, Rakuten Advertising, and Shopify merchants). If you tap an affiliate product link and purchase on an external merchant website, the merchant or network may place cookies or track the referral to credit the affiliate purchase. We do not share your private wardrobe photos or personal chat history with these third-party merchants.

08

Data Retention, Backup Cycles & Direct Account Deletion

We retain your information only as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete control over your data:

  • You can edit or delete individual clothing items and outfits at any time directly in your closet view.
  • You can permanently delete your entire account, profile, wardrobe photos, and messages at any time by going to Settings > Delete Account. Upon entering your password or verifying deletion via authenticated link, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted from our active databases and file storage.
  • Backup Lifecycle (Spec Ch5 §1.3): Encrypted database snapshots generated for disaster recovery and business continuity are retained for a rolling window of exactly 35 days, after which they are irreversibly overwritten and purged.
09

Data Security

We implement robust industry-standard technical and organizational security measures—including TLS/SSL transport encryption, token-based authentication, and restricted database access—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

10

International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have statutory rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, port, or request erasure of your data, or to restrict or object to certain processing activities. You may exercise these rights directly within the app settings or by contacting our Data Protection team.

11

Children's Privacy

Closly is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.
